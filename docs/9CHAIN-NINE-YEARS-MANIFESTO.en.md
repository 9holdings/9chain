# Nine years, nine billion — one blockchain for every person in the age of AI

*A document for the 9Chain community. English edition, `2026-09-04`. Vietnamese original: `9CHAIN-NINE-YEARS-MANIFESTO.md`.*

---

## The ninth of September

On `2026-09-09` we engrave two sentences into the first block of 9Chain — Block Adam — and start counting nine
years. Nine years later, on `2035-09-09`, the United Nations expects the Earth to hold close to nine billion
people. 9Chain's goal for those nine years is that **every one of them owns a blockchain of their own**.

Not because blockchains are beautiful. Because something is happening to all of us.

---

## 1. What is happening

More than a billion people use AI every week. Today AI **answers**. Tomorrow AI **acts**: it books, pays bills,
negotiates, signs, buys, and represents you in front of someone else's AI. That is good news. It also raises a
question humanity has never had to answer:

> **What is my AI allowed to do — and who holds the evidence?**

Today the answer is: *the company that sells you the AI*. They hold the permissions, they hold the logs, they are
the referee when something goes wrong. That is fine while AI only answers. It is not fine when AI spends your money,
signs on your behalf, and when many AIs from many companies all work for the same you. A prompt that says *"don't
spend more than $20"* is not a limit — an AI can be tricked, persuaded, injected. **A real limit must live outside
the AI, in a place the AI cannot edit, and it must belong to you.**

That place is a blockchain of your own.

---

## 2. Five promises to every person

A blockchain of your own is not a technical object. It is five promises:

| | Promise | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Sovereignty** | Nobody — not even 9Chain — can edit, delete, or lock your ledger. Its rules are the rules you set. |
| **2** | **Permanence** | Losing your phone does not lose your ledger. It outlives every device and every company. |
| **3** | **Verifiability** | You cannot quietly rewrite the past, and anyone can check that. Your evidence is not held by the other side. |
| **4** | **Portability** | Change your host, your provider, your country — your name and history come with you. |
| **5** | **Interoperability** | Your ledger talks to other people's ledgers, to shops, to communities, to the world. |

There is one promise we do **not** make: *"your chain will run forever."* It does not need to. Your ledger sleeps
when you sleep. Anyone who needs to read it reads the anchored copy. If your AI must work while you sleep, it works
wherever you choose — the keys stay yours.

---

## 3. A digital constitution: AI must ask

Your blockchain carries a **constitution** — not a document, but a contract that runs:

- **Which AI** may sign for you; each AI gets its own key.
- **What it may do**, up to **how much**, until **when**.
- **When it must ask again** — cross the threshold and it stops and asks you.
- **Revoke** in one tap, with nobody's permission.
- **Every action leaves a receipt** — signed, timestamped, attributed, non-repudiable.

The constitution is a contract, not a prompt — because the only thing an AI cannot talk its way past is a contract.
We did not invent a new language for it: 9Chain's constitution speaks the standards the world is converging on —
AP2 mandates, scoped permissions from ERC-7715, agent identity from ERC-8004, payments over x402, credentials as
W3C Verifiable Credentials. Any company's AI can understand your ledger.

---

## 4. What a blockchain of your own looks like

It has your name: `lan.9s-union.love9`. It has your rules written into its first block. It has its own token as fuel
— not for trading, for running. It records three things: **commitments**, **permissions**, **receipts**. It does
**not** contain your private data — photos, medical records, messages stay outside, encrypted, under your key; the
ledger only holds proof that they exist and who may see them. That is how you keep your full right to delete, exactly
as personal-data law protects it.

And you never need to learn the word "blockchain". No seed phrase. No "gas". No 0x address. A sixty-year-old must be
able to create a ledger, grant an AI one permission, and take it back — with nobody standing beside them. That is our
yardstick.

### One day, in 2028

`06:30` — Lan's AI pays the electricity bill from a capped wallet; the receipt lands in Lan's ledger. `08:00` — the
café signs a receipt into Lan's ledger; cashback is evidence, not a promise. `10:00` — the AI tries to book a flight
above its limit: the constitution refuses, the phone asks, Lan nods with a fingerprint. `14:00` — a clinic asks to
read a test result; Lan grants 24 hours; the ledger records who read what and when. `18:00` — Lan presents a
membership credential to enter an event, without revealing a phone number. `23:00` — the ledger anchors to the
community chain; Lan sleeps, the chain sleeps. **At no point does Lan see the word "blockchain".**

## 5. A tree, not a tower

Nine billion blockchains do not sit on one network. They grow on a **tree**:

```
ROOT     root network       — small, durable, boring; keeps names and anchors. 9Chain is one root among many.
TRUNK    regional network   — of a region, a country, an alliance of communities.
BRANCH   community chain    — of a club, a school, a company, a neighbourhood; run by that community itself.
LEAF     personal ledger    — yours. Nine billion leaves.
```

Each level is the **registry** and the **anchor point** for the level below. No level has to re-run the level below
— it only **verifies proofs**. Names follow the tree: `lan.9s-union.mekong.love9`. The tree grows by adding branches,
not by making the root thicker. That is the only way to nine billion, and it is built on the open Avalanche
technology 9Chain inherits: every chain has its own validator set, which can be a single device owned by its owner;
chains talk through signed messages; a chain's rules live in its genesis.

others carry it further than we could — and that is the plan.

## 7. Nine challenges we choose to solve

Nine years is long, and nine billion is many. Here are nine problems, and how we walk:

1. **Make it invisible.** Nobody wants "a blockchain", just as nobody wanted HTTPS. People want an AI that does not
   overspend and receipts that are theirs. The chain sits underneath, unseen, like DNS.
2. **Keep it under one dollar per person per year.** Ledgers sleep when idle; one ledger host serves thousands like
   a mail server. This is an engineering target, not a hope.
3. **A registry for nine billion.** One network can register twenty thousand chains; nine billion needs a tree.
   Recursion through contracts, regional networks, and a protocol improvement that 9Chain will try first and
   contribute back to Avalanche.
4. **Real people, without 9Chain asking for papers.** Governments are issuing identity wallets; communities know
   each other's faces. The ledger accepts credentials; it collects no data.
5. **The law as an ally.** Europe's AI Act requires logging every action of high-risk AI from August 2026; Vietnam's
   personal-data law protects the right to delete. A personal ledger answers both. We ask lawyers first, not after.
6. **AI platforms opening to an external root.** We build a tool interface so any AI can call your ledger: ask
   permission, log a receipt, check a mandate. AIs integrate what has a tool interface.
7. **An open standard, many roots.** Specification before code; open source, in English; three independent
   implementations before we call it a standard. 9Chain is the first root, not the only one.
8. **Communities running nodes for each other.** Your chain is kept by your people, on home machines, old phones,
   small boxes — one validator seat is a cluster of devices, and one of them sleeping is fine. 9Chain lends nodes
   for the first thirty days, then steps back.
9. **Outliving us.** Names are permanent, never deleted; chains hibernate instead of closing; governance of the
   standard moves to the community on a schedule published in advance.

## 8. What we commit not to do

- **We do not keep your data.** The ledger keeps evidence, not your private life.
- **We do not ask for your papers.** Identity is between you and your community.
- **We do not delete.** Names, ledgers, history — permanent; they sleep, they do not die.
- **We do not make you buy a token to have a ledger.** The standard is open; LOVE9 is fuel for the 9Chain root
  network, not a ticket at the door.
- **We do not build our own AI.** We stay neutral so every AI can serve you on the same ledger.
